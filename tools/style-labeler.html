Your allowed styles, not the game’s defaults

Selections start empty. A style can be allowed for Male, Female, or Shared. Shared means both genders. Selecting both Male and Female is automatically grouped as Shared. Unselected styles are excluded from both pools.

Hair and accessories have separate lists. “None” is always a safe fallback for head accessories. These choices do not change your league yet.

Your selections are saved in this browser when storage is available. Export a TXT backup before switching browsers or computers.
